**Welcome. Velkommen. Willkommen. 欢迎。**

Whether you have followed the channel for a while or have just found your way here, welcome to Linux Observatory.

I'm Ediz, based in Copenhagen, Denmark. I explore games on Linux, from hidden gems to demanding worlds, at 4K and beyond. The recordings follow the game continuously, with live performance overlays showing the smooth moments, the stutters and everything in between.

**Linux gameplay worth watching. Real performance worth observing. Tux with a telescope.**

Today, that telescope has a few more welcome signs beside it.

The website began in English. As it grew, a simple question came up: could more people feel at home here if the introduction were available in their own language?

That became the next step: adding **Danish, German and Simplified Chinese**, while keeping English as the original.

Danish brings a little of my home in Copenhagen into the site. German and Simplified Chinese open the introduction to more readers beyond it. You should be able to find out what this project is about without having to work through an unfamiliar language first.

We started with the three pages that introduce the project: the homepage, About and Start Here. Their text, navigation and explanations now have translated versions, including the homepage's interactive example of frame times.

The familiar design stays. So do the game names, technical identifiers, recording links and performance values. MangoHud is still MangoHud. The numbers still describe the same recordings.

On a first visit to an English entry page, the site can use your browser's language preferences to choose a supported translation. The visible language selector lets you choose for yourself and remembers that choice. Switching languages keeps you on the corresponding page, and direct links to translated pages stay in that language.

This is a first step. The blog—including this post—and the other sections remain in English. If a translation sounds awkward or a technical explanation could be clearer, please [let me know](/contact/). Useful feedback can help make these pages more welcoming.

If you're new here, you don't need to understand every number in the overlay. Start with a game that interests you. Watch for a few minutes. Notice how it moves, where it pauses and what changes on screen. The [Start Here guide](/start-here/) is there when you'd like a little context.

And if you've been here before, thank you for watching, asking questions and taking an interest in this exploration of Linux gaming.

[Explore the gameplay on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@LinuxObservatory), or [join the conversation on Discord](https://discord.gg/HrbFbM3VKD). Game suggestions and curious questions are welcome.

Whatever language brought you here, I'm glad you found the observatory.

— Ediz
