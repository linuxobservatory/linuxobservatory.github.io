What a number can—and cannot—tell you.

Common performance overlay metrics, meanings and limitations
ReadoutWhat it describesWhat it does not prove
FPSFrames produced per second over the measurement intervalThat every frame arrived evenly
Frame time (ms)Time associated with individual framesThe specific reason a frame was slow
1% lowA summary of slower frame delivery, calculated by a defined methodExactly when or why a hitch occurred; calculation methods vary
GPU utilizationEstimated GPU engine activityWhich exact subsystem is limiting the workload
VRAM use / allocationMemory a game or system requests, reserves or uses depending on the sensorThat the same amount is actively occupied by unique texture data
Power / temperatureReported operating conditionsThat temperature alone caused a slowdown
When 100 FPS doesn't feel like 100 FPS

Averaging can conceal intermittent slow frames. A frame-time trace, capture context and repeat observation are more useful than one headline number for diagnosing a hitch.
