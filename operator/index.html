EDIZ INIZIEL

Why I started recording.

I'm Ediz Iniziel, a Linux enthusiast and PC gamer based in Copenhagen, Denmark. I run Linux Observatory independently, from choosing games and configuring the system to recording and publishing the results.

The idea grew out of something simple. I was following Linux gaming coverage, including GamingOnLinux, and noticed that many interesting games and Steam demos were being written about without much accompanying gameplay footage. I was already using Linux and loved it, so I thought: why not play those games, record what actually happens, and share it?

What began as a way to show games running on Linux became a broader interest in observing the details: compatibility, graphics drivers, Proton, Mesa, performance overlays, frame pacing and the behavior of games under unusual workloads. Sometimes that means ordinary gameplay. Sometimes it means pushing rendering resolutions far beyond 4K just to see what happens.

I handle the work myself: selecting workloads, maintaining the test environment, configuring instrumentation, recording continuous gameplay and publishing observations. The operating system and software stack can change over time; as of November 2026, I've switched to Fedora 45.

I don't want every recording to be mistaken for a controlled benchmark. A video shows what happened in that particular run, under those particular conditions. That is why I keep the footage central: The footage is the evidence. Testing is the product.

Linux Observatory is independent and unsponsored. If you have a game or demo you'd like me to explore, you can reach me through the Linux Observatory YouTube channel.
