OPERATOR / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

One operator.
Many observations.

Linux Observatory is operated by Ediz Iniziel, based in Copenhagen, Denmark. The project’s method is personal and hands-on rather than institutional.

EDIZ INIZIEL

The work behind the recording.

Ediz Iniziel plans the workloads, maintains the test environment, runs games, configures overlays, records footage and publishes the observations.

His areas of practical focus include Linux gaming compatibility, graphics drivers, Proton, Mesa RADV, on-screen performance metrics, frame pacing and experimental rendering beyond a physical monitor’s native resolution.

The testing environment has evolved between Linux distributions and software versions; Fedora 45 is the latest operating system reported by the operator as of November 2026.

Linux Observatory is described as independent and unsponsored. It is not presented as a university laboratory, an official Mesa or Valve testing group, or an external certification body.

To discuss an observation or suggest a future game, use the public Linux Observatory YouTube channel.
