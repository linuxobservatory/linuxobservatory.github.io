For questions about a game, driver change, recording or experimental condition, use the comments or publicly available contact details on the official YouTube channel. This website intentionally does not invent an email address, form or social profile.

How to cite a recording

Ediz Iniziel / Linux Observatory.
"[Exact recording title]" (YouTube, [upload date]).
Test conditions: [as documented in that recording].
[Direct original video URL]. Accessed [date].

A citation should link to the original recording, preserve the project name and identify the relevant conditions. Do not present an observational run as a controlled scientific result unless the underlying protocol supports that description.

Redistribution

The operator’s stated preferences are to preserve attribution, keep Linux Observatory branding intact, link prominently to the source and avoid misleading retitling or repackaging. These preferences are not a blanket open-content license. Contact the rights holder where formal reuse permission is required.
