INDEPENDENT & PUBLIC

The footage stays open.

All footage and findings remain publicly available. Support helps fund the work; you do not need to pay to watch, learn or join the discussion.

A contribution does not purchase a particular test, a deadline or a favourable result. Game suggestions are welcome from everyone, and I choose what to investigate independently.

Observe. Instrument. Record. Inspect. The same approach applies whether you contribute or simply watch.
